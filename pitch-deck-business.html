Why States Are Pushing Back

  • Hyperscale campuses overwhelm local power grids
  • Massive water consumption for cooling (millions of gallons/day)
  • Communities see burden without proportional economic benefit
  • Environmental impact reviews are blocking permits
  • Political pressure from residents and local government

What This Concentration Means

  • Critical internet functions have no geographic redundancy
  • No data sovereignty — state/local governments have zero control over where their data lives
  • AI inference requires low latency that centralized facilities cannot serve at the edge
  • A single regional disaster could degrade federal, financial, and defense systems simultaneously

Data sovereignty can only be addressed by local operation — no amount of cloud policy can substitute for physical proximity.

Why Cell Tower Sites

Existing Power & Connectivity

Tower sites already have utility power feeds and fiber/microwave backhaul. We scale to the grid capacity that's already there — no new substations or transmission lines needed.

Permitting Risk Eliminated

By staying inside the existing site footprint, we operate under the tower's existing land use permits and zoning. No new environmental reviews, no public hearings, no 2-year permitting battles.

Geographic Distribution Built-In

Cell towers are sited for population coverage. We inherit a deployment map that already optimizes for proximity to end users — exactly what edge AI inference requires.

AI Native = Lower Operating Cost

Making the micro datacenter AI-native from the ground up means autonomous operations: AI handles provisioning, monitoring, billing, and maintenance. 1 human operator per 20-50 sites, not 5-10 humans per site.

Keys to Success & Risk Mitigation

Keys to Success

Speed to Scale

Prefab modular units deploy in 60-90 days vs. 18-24 months for traditional builds. Cell tower sites provide a pre-validated pipeline of 40,000+ locations. Target: 3-5 sites in Year 1, 50 by Year 2, 300 by Year 4.

Cost Management

AI-native operations are the economic unlock. Traditional sub-2MW sites need $400K+/yr in staffing alone. ANDD's AI ops model: $80K-$150K/yr OpEx per site. Power scales to existing grid — no infrastructure buildout premiums.

Compute Fill at Premium Rates

Inference-specific positioning commands premium pricing: customers pay for low-latency guarantees and data sovereignty, not commodity compute. AI capacity planning ensures utilization by routing workloads across the mesh to fill available capacity.

Offtake Customers

Government contracts (3-5 yr, premium rates) provide base revenue. AI companies and healthcare provide volume. Multi-rail revenue — subscriptions, usage-based, micro-transactions — diversifies customer concentration risk.

Key Risks & Mitigations

Crown Castle Partnership

Medium

Risk: Agreement may not materialize or terms may be unfavorable.
Mitigation: Cell towers are one channel. ANDD can co-locate at any commercial site with power and fiber — industrial parks, substations, ISP POPs. Multiple site strategies in parallel.

Utilization / Fill Rate

Medium

Risk: Sites don't reach target utilization fast enough.
Mitigation: AI demand modeling drives site selection. Government anchor tenants provide base load. Mesh routing fills spare capacity from adjacent regions.

Technology Execution

Low

Risk: AI management layer doesn't achieve target autonomy.
Mitigation: Each component (monitoring, billing, provisioning) is proven technology. The innovation is integration, not invention. Graduated rollout with human fallback.

Competition

Low

Risk: Hyperscalers or incumbents replicate the model.
Mitigation: Hyperscalers are optimized for 100MW+ campuses — sub-2MW is structurally unattractive for them. Previous edge attempts (Vapor IO, EdgeMicro) failed on economics that AI ops now solve.

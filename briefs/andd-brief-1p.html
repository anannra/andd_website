The offering in one page

Compute within 10 milliseconds, sovereignty by construction, operated end to end by AI.

ANDD is a mesh of sub-2MW edge datacenters in Opportunity Zone metros, run by autonomous AI agents at a ratio of one human per 20-50 sites. We sell the three things that don't deflate as token prices fall: milliseconds, jurisdiction, and reliability. Tokens bring customers onto the network; locality, sovereignty, and self-executing SLAs carry the margin.
Proximity
Heavy GPUs under 10ms from mid-size metros. No provider prices latency by geography today; AWS's 25% Local Zone uplift proves buyers pay for it.
Sovereignty
Jurisdiction-pinned placement is a signup checkbox: attested residency, compliance bundles, survive-in-place autonomy.
Zero-touch operations
The AI that runs the datacenters runs the funnel: quote to provisioned workload in minutes, every deal under $250k TCV closed without a human.

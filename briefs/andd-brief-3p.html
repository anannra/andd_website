01 · Why we win

Compute within 10 milliseconds, sovereignty by construction, operated end to end by AI.

ANDD is a mesh of sub-2MW edge datacenters in Opportunity Zone metros, run by autonomous AI agents at a ratio of one human per 20-50 sites. Token prices have fallen roughly 99% since 2023 and keep falling, so we never anchor margin to $/token. The durable premiums attach to what does not deflate: milliseconds (physics), jurisdiction (law), reliability (engineering), and compliance (regulation). Tokens bring customers onto the network; locality, sovereignty, and self-executing SLAs carry the margin.
Proximity
Heavy GPUs under 10ms from mid-size metros, sold as a priced, SLA-backed tier. No provider prices latency by geography today.
Sovereignty
Jurisdiction-pinned placement as a checkbox: attested residency, compliance bundles, air-gap and survive-in-place autonomy.
Zero-touch operations
The AI that runs the datacenters runs the funnel: quote to provisioned workload in minutes, every deal under $250k TCV closed without a human.

StorageSTO

NetworkNET
Private interconnect into premises and cloud on-ramps (AWS DX, Azure ER, GCP), carrier-neutral peering, metro wavelengths, CDN with semantic AI-response caching, zero-trust mesh VPN across a customer's footprint.

DePIN & Web3DPN
Validator hosting with anti-correlation guarantees (no two validators share a node or power region), managed RPC, verifiable compute with TEE attestations, and programmable SLAs (5% attach) whose stablecoin credits cascade into a protocol's own commitments.
Whole-nodeNOD

MarketplaceMKT
Prepaid credits from $5 to $50k (card, ACH, USDC provisioned within one block), per-call pay-as-you-go with no subscription or minimum, a public machine-readable spot exchange, and in Phase 3 a model hub (ANDD takes 15-20%) and agent-template store.

SLAs & billing
gated by physics, enforced by code

Every SLA is code. Credits land automatically with no claims process; breach telemetry is co-signed by an independent probe vendor, so customers audit us rather than trust us. Tiers are gated on node density: one node contracts 99.9%, a diversity-audited metro pair 99.99%, and five nines is published as measured, never contracted in this horizon. Confirmed data loss pays 12 months of storage fees; a residency violation pays 100% of the monthly charge.

The commercial engine
reservations anchor, commits fund, usage fills

Anchor-tenant node launches. Every node opens its anchor book at site shortlisting: 40-60% of capacity pre-sold before ground-break at the best pricing that node will ever see (typically 45-55% off list, 5-year terms), with delivery-date SLAs enforced by the smart-contract machinery. A half-pre-sold node presents to QOF investors as contracted infrastructure, lowering the cost of capital. The rest is the high-margin merchant layer sold through PLG and commits.

The offering and the opening

Compute within 10 milliseconds, sovereignty by construction, operated end to end by AI.

ANDD is a mesh of sub-2MW edge datacenters in Opportunity Zone metros, run by autonomous AI agents at a ratio of one human per 20-50 sites. Token prices have fallen roughly 99% since 2023 and keep falling, so we never anchor margin to $/token. The durable premiums attach to what does not deflate: milliseconds (physics), jurisdiction (law), reliability (engineering), and compliance (regulation). Tokens bring customers onto the network; locality, sovereignty, and self-executing SLAs carry the margin.
Proximity
Heavy GPUs under 10ms from mid-size metros, sold as a priced, SLA-backed tier. No provider prices latency by geography today.
Sovereignty
Jurisdiction-pinned placement as a checkbox: attested residency, compliance bundles, air-gap and survive-in-place autonomy.
Zero-touch operations
The AI that runs the datacenters runs the funnel: quote to provisioned workload in minutes, every deal under $250k TCV closed without a human.

SLAs & billing
gated by physics, enforced by code

Every SLA is code. Credits land automatically with no claims process, and breach telemetry is co-signed by an independent probe vendor, so customers audit us rather than trust us. Tiers are gated on node density per metro: a single node contracts 99.9%, a diversity-audited metro pair 99.99%, and five nines is published as measured, never contracted in this horizon. Latency SLAs cover p99 within published service polygons drawn from measured data.

The commercial engine
reservations anchor, commits fund, usage fills

Anchor-tenant node launches. Every node opens its anchor book at site shortlisting: 40-60% of capacity pre-sold before ground-break at the best pricing that node will ever see (typically 45-55% off list, 5-year terms), with delivery-date SLAs enforced by the same smart-contract machinery. A half-pre-sold node presents to QOF investors as contracted infrastructure, which lowers the cost of capital. The remaining capacity is the high-margin merchant layer.

Unit economics, 300kW inference node (about $8M all-in, roughly 180 L40S-class accelerators): breakeven near 23-25% utilization; cash margin 43% at 40% utilization, 60% at 60%, 68% at 80%. GAAP profitability arrives around 45-50% utilization. Blended gross margin runs 45-50% in Phase 2 toward 60%+ at maturity. Commit discounts: 1-year 25-35%, 3-year 40-50%; take-or-pay floors at 70-85% with a remarketing clause.

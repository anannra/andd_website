Inference is moving to the edge. Latency tolerance is gone.

The shift from building models to deploying them is structural, and it is colliding with a hard constraint: existing megawatts at the right location.

Inference dominates compute

Inference is now the majority of AI compute and growing faster than training. The shift from model-building to model-deployment is structural and accelerating.

Latency tolerance is gone

Real-time voice, agentic sessions, inline security, and physical AI cannot tolerate a distant round trip. A rising share of workloads demands local, sub-20 ms response.

Hyperscale is the wrong architecture

Hyperscale was engineered for centralized, batch-oriented training, not for distributed, low-latency inference. Repurposing it is not a retrofit. It is a rebuild.

Hyperscale supply has stalled

Power and water scarcity, permitting backlogs, and community opposition stall large builds for years. New capacity cannot arrive in time to meet the inference wave.

The edge layer is unowned

The distributed, low-latency inference layer does not exist yet. Real-time voice, agentic AI, inline security, and physical AI have nowhere to run at the edge. That is what we build.

Modular, low-friction AI datacenters sited where power already exists.

AI-native modular design

Sub-20 MW datacenters built from two pre-defined modules: the EC500 at 500 kW and the EC1000 at 1 MW. A facility starts small and expands to 20 MW as demand is contracted. Capital follows commitment, not speculation.

Sited on existing and stranded power

We site where utility capacity already exists: co-ops, municipals, cell sites, investor-owned utilities, and RTOs. Rates are locked and interconnect expedited before a single rack is deployed.

Minimal friction

No community water draw. Minimal noise profile. Expedited permitting through relocatable precast foundations. The form factor removes the slow parts by engineering them out.

Land-to-power pipeline exceeds 1 GW today

Faster, quieter, closer, and cheaper. With a mechanism behind each claim.

Live in 90 to 180 days

Relocatable precast foundations eliminate the concrete cure cycle. EC500 and EC1000 blocks arrive pre-engineered. No long-lead custom construction. The design is the speed.

No community conflict

Zero community water draw. Minimal acoustic footprint. Expedited permitting through relocatable structures. We do not need years of community relations before breaking ground.

Under 30 ms at the edge

Carrier-adjacent siting removes the backhaul hop. Bonded fiber with Starlink as diverse backup delivers the latency profile real-time AI workloads require.

Lower capital, leaner operations

Roughly half the capital per megawatt of a traditional hyperscale build, delivered five to ten times faster. AI-native operations keep the operator-to-site ratio lean.

Inference is moving to the edge, and the edge layer is unowned.

Inference dominates compute. It is now the majority of AI compute and growing faster than training. The shift from model-building to model-deployment is structural and accelerating.

Latency tolerance is gone. Real-time voice, agentic sessions, inline security, and physical AI cannot tolerate a distant round trip. A rising share of workloads demands local, sub-20 ms response.

Hyperscale cannot fill the demand. It was engineered for centralized, batch-oriented training, and new capacity is stalled by power and water scarcity, permitting backlogs, and community opposition.

So the layer does not exist yet. Real-time voice, agentic AI, inline security, and physical AI have nowhere to run at the edge. That is what we build.

Two modules, one template, sited on existing power.
